Why this works

You are getting guidance from someone who has been inside the process.

Most career advice comes from people who have looked for a job. Mine comes from more than 20 years on the other side of the table. I have been the person reading the stack of resumes, sitting on the interview panel, and in the room when the hiring decision actually gets made, as a certified HR professional and leadership consultant across corporate, non-profit, and growing organizations.

What I kept seeing was the same thing: capable people losing out on roles they could have done well, because no one had ever explained how the process works from the inside. Nobody tells you that a resume gets about twenty seconds of attention, or that the answer you thought was humble read as unprepared. That gap is what HR Expert to go exists to close.

"The kindest thing I can do is tell you the truth about where you stand. Confidence comes from being prepared, not from being reassured."

The part of this work I like most tends to show up late. Someone emails months or even years later to say they got the role, took the promotion, or finally found work they actually enjoy. That is usually preparation catching up with them.

How a discovery call works

Twenty minutes. No agenda except figuring out what you actually need.

A short conversation to understand where you are and whether I am the right person to help. Bring whatever you have, even if that is a rough resume and a lot of questions.

  • We start with you. What is your situation right now, what have you tried, and what feels stuck.
  • We get specific. Whether the biggest blocker is your resume, your interviews, your search strategy, or something else entirely.
  • I give you a clear recommendation. The right next step for your situation, not a packaged program that may or may not fit.
  • If there is a fit, we talk about how to work together. If there is not, I will tell you that too and point you in a better direction.

One thing to know

The call is for figuring out fit and direction, so there is not time to review a resume line by line on it. That work happens in a paid session, and you will know by the end of the call whether it is worth booking one.
